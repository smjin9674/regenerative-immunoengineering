Research Interests

  • Aging and regenerative niche biology: Defining how aging reshapes immune–stromal–vascular interactions that govern repair, fibrosis, and regeneration.
  • Multi-omics-guided therapeutic discovery: Translating disease- and repair-associated cell states, pathways, and spatiotemporal cues into biomaterial-based and immunomodulatory strategies.
  • AI/ML-enabled regenerative medicine: Integrating multi-omics datasets, prioritizing therapeutic targets, and designing next-generation regenerative modalities.
