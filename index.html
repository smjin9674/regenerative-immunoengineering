01 / Research

Research directions

Three connected research focuses link mechanistic biology with translational design.

Focus I

Aging, Regeneration & Tissue Niche Biology

Understanding how aging reshapes immune, stromal, and vascular interactions to shift tissue repair from regeneration toward fibrosis.

Focus II

Multicellular Niche & Therapeutic Target Discovery

Integrating single-cell and spatial multi-omics, cell–cell communication analysis, network modeling, and AI to uncover causal interactions and therapeutic vulnerabilities within tissue microenvironments.

Focus III

Spatiotemporal Niche Reprogramming

Engineering biomaterials and dynamic immunomodulatory strategies to control when and where therapies act and restore regenerative tissue states.
