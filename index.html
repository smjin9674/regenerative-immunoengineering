01 / About

A multiscale view of regeneration

Regeneration is not a single-cell problem. I investigate how aging alters immune, stromal, and vascular interactions across space and time. By integrating multi-omics with biomaterial and immunomodulatory design, I aim to identify mechanisms we can measure—and interventions we can build.

02 / Research

Research directions

Four connected programs link mechanistic biology with translational design.

01

Aging & regenerative niche biology

Defining how aging reshapes immune–stromal–vascular interactions that govern tissue repair, fibrosis, and regeneration after severe injury.

Aging · Tissue repair · Regenerative niches

02

Immunomodulatory biomaterials

Engineering materials and delivery systems that control the timing, location, and duration of immune signals for durable therapeutic responses.

Biomaterials · Immune engineering · Drug delivery

03

Multi-omics therapeutic discovery

Translating disease- and repair-associated cell states, signaling pathways, and spatiotemporal cues into testable therapeutic strategies.

Single-cell · Spatial omics · Systems biology

04

AI-guided therapeutic discovery

Integrating multi-omics datasets, prioritizing therapeutic targets, and designing next-generation regenerative modalities with AI/ML.

Single-cell · Spatial omics · Machine learning
