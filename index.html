01 / About

A multiscale view of regeneration

Regeneration is not a single-cell problem. I investigate how material cues, immune-state transitions, tissue architecture, and aging interact across scales. The aim is to identify mechanisms we can measure—and interventions we can build.

02 / Research

Research directions

Four connected programs link mechanistic biology with translational design.

01

Biomaterials for tissue repair

Designing instructive materials that shape cell recruitment, inflammation, vascularization, and durable tissue restoration.

Biomaterials · Drug delivery · Tissue engineering

02

Regenerative immunology

Resolving how innate and adaptive immune programs influence repair—and how those programs can be redirected toward regeneration.

Macrophages · Inflammation · Immune engineering

03

Aging & volumetric muscle loss

Studying why severe muscle injuries fail to heal, with particular attention to age-dependent changes in the regenerative niche.

VML · Skeletal muscle · Aging

04

AI-guided therapeutic discovery

Combining single-cell and spatial measurements with computational models to prioritize mechanisms and design testable interventions.

Single-cell · Spatial omics · Machine learning
